## Finding 1 — Authenticity Guarantee + Verified / Mall Badging

- **Platform:** DarazMall (BD) + Pickaboo (BD specialist) + Bikroy Verified Badge — validated [WS]
- **Market Context:** Bangladesh e-commerce penetration only 2–3% of retail (TBS 2026 interview with Daraz Group CCO Ben Yi). Scam wave early 2020s "really shook confidence" (Daraz CCO Rusho, Financial Express). Trust — not price — is the #1 barrier; Levree 2026 guide explicitly advises buyers to vet seller rating >4.0 and verify warranty documentation before trusting platform-level 4.5 aggregate.
- **Pattern:** Marketplace layer separates *verified brand supply* from general sellers. DarazMall: sellers must be brand owners/importers, carry **Mall tag**, flagship store, `Authenticity Guarantee — replacement + 3× cash back if fake`, and 14-day hassle-free returns (expanded from Mall-only to ALL products in 2025). Pickaboo parallel: `100% genuine 400+ brands + official manufacturer warranty + 0 tolerance for duplicates`. Bikroy variant: `Verified Badge` eligibility requires **6 months consecutive membership + email/business name + NID/Trade License verification** (field sales executive verified) to display badge on shop. Star Tech variant: `20+ physical stores + ISO 9001:2015 + 64-district coverage + 15 service centers` as physical trust proxy.
- **Problem Solved:** "Original Product Guaranteed?" homepage trust bar `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:34` and footer claims have no PDP-level proof. PDP `TRUST-01 P2` (`02-ux-audit/issue-register.md:35`) notes trust built at entry dissipates at consideration — no Mall badge, no verified-seller identity, no warranty provenance on PDP where Tk 81k–1.38L decision happens (`01-current-state/product-page-variations.md:54-55`).
- **Why It Is Relevant:** Transcom IS the brand-authorized electronics retailer (like Pickaboo/Star Tech), not a marketplace — authenticity is its natural moat vs Daraz marketplace variance. Yet PDP currently shows generic `Brand link → /brand/{brand}` (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:112`) with no `Authorized / Mall / Verified — Official Warranty` lockup, no `Authenticity Guarantee` details link, and no seller-rating analogue. `ecommerce-capabilities.md:46` notes warranty line `Special Component-60M` but without authorized-source framing, it reads as claim not proof.
- **Applicability:** **HIGH** — reuses existing brand partnerships (Samsung, Daikin, Haier etc. 13 brands `00-input/sitemap-analysis.md:26`). PDP hero should carry `✓ Authorized — Official Warranty` + warranty detail sheet + `Authenticity: if proven fake, replacement + 3× back` adapted from DarazMall. Suppressed for brand-owned PDPs where self-evident; critical for multi-brand trust at price exposure.
- **Classification:** **INDUSTRY STANDARD** in Bangladesh marketplace context; **DIFFERENTIATOR** when retailer proves authorization at PDP (vs marketplace badge). Absence is parity gap vs Pickaboo/Star Tech.

## Finding 2 — OTP-Verified & Open-Box Delivery at Doorstep

- **Platform:** Flipkart Open Box Delivery + OTP-based authentication + Flipkart × India Post COD parcel integration (1.6 lakh post offices) — verified May 2026 DoP agreement + Flipkart OBD T&C [WS]
- **Market Context:** Cash on Delivery remains **75–90% of transactions** in Bangladesh (Levree 2026); delivery fraud ("wrong item / damage claimed after rider left") is endemic in South Asia. Flipkart solved with two controls: (a) **Open Box** — Wishmaster opens *both outer + brand packing in front of customer* before handover, checks damage / correct item / IMEI / accessories; (b) **OTP authentication** — customer shares OTP only after satisfaction, logged with photo documentation. India Post deal explicitly covers COD with OTP + real-time tracking.
- **Pattern:** High-value categories (>~₹5k / Tk 5k) default to OBD *where pincode eligible*: option offered on Order Summary page (`If your order is eligible for OBD, you will get option to select Open Box Delivery in the Order Summary page` — Flipkart T&C). Rejection grounds standardized: physical damage, wrong item, IMEI mismatch, missing accessories. Refusal is photographed and logged; reference number required before rider leaves. Trust Shield (Sept 2025) extends to 30-day accidental + liquid protection with single-point contact — but OBD is the doorstep prevention, Trust Shield is post-purchase remediation.
- **Problem Solved:** Transcom PDP `PDP-02 P1` location gate hides deliverability, but even when deliverable, big-ticket AC 1 Ton / 600L fridge / 65" TV has highest "what if wrong/damaged at door?" anxiety. `ecommerce-capabilities.md:42-44` delivery preview is gated; no OBD or OTP language exists. Current experience puts documentation burden on buyer *after* self-unboxing (Amazon India contrast noted by TechEnclave: Amazon no OBD → return process burden on buyer).
- **Why It Is Relevant:** Bangladesh COD + large-appliance delivery to districts outside Dhaka (5–8 day window outside metros per Levree) amplifies doorstep-dispute risk. Transcom's owned logistics + Store Locator network (`/store-locator` VERIFIED List/Map + Mapbox `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:199-204`) gives credible Wishmaster equivalent vs pure marketplace. Order value 50k–1.5L justifies OBD cost; "Original Product Guaranteed" claim (`page-analysis.md:34`) is materially strengthened when customer physically verifies brand pack at handover.
- **Applicability:** **HIGH** — offer OBD as *opt-in* on Checkout Order Summary for electronics >Tk 20k, with OTP verification. Document with photo + reference (prevents TechEnclave failure mode: rider repacks mid-inspection). Pair with Finding 5 serviceability check: `Eligible for Open Box Delivery at your pincode ✓` badge.
- **Classification:** **INDUSTRY STANDARD** in India electronics; **EMERGING** in Bangladesh (Daraz not yet OBD-standard; Pickaboo Star Tech not documented). Early adoption is regional differentiator for large appliances.

## Finding 3 — Multi-Bank EMI36 with Per-Product Eligibility, Tenure Picker & Offline Form Flow

- **Platform:** Pickaboo 0% EMI + Daraz EMI — both BD-verified [WS]
- **Market Context:** Bangladesh electronics is financed via **credit-card EMI** (not BNPL debit) through bank partnership network. Pickaboo lists **32 banks** (City AMEX, SCB, EBL, BRAC, LankaBangla, DBBL etc.) with per-product tenure visibility on PDP + Checkout; threshold **BDT 5,000** min product price. Daraz: BDT 10,000 min per product (not cart total), EMI form rider sent 7–10 working days post-delivery, customer calls 16492 to convert. Both emphasize EMI *per individual product* — not cart aggregate. Formal process: full amount blocked on card → signed EMI form submitted (Pickaboo: within 3 working days at showroom) → bank converts in 5–10 working days → EMI charged from next billing cycle.
- **Pattern:** PDP displays tenure range **up to 36 months** (`EMI36` badge on PLP cards `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:87` is BD standard), plus `EMI From X Tk/month` with bank list. Checkout `Payment Information` shows bank × tenure × monthly matrix. Offline touchpoint: physical EMI form dispatch (Daraz: rider brings form post-delivery; Pickaboo: in-store form). Transparency on failure: Daraz explicitly notes Dhaka-only for 0% EMI, and 7–10 day reflection; Pickaboo notes tenure varies per product type.
- **Problem Solved:** Transcom `CONSISTENCY-01 P3` / `PDP-06 P2` (`02-ux-audit/issue-register.md:31,44`): EMI badge `EMI36` on PLP diverges from PDP text `Avail Bank EMI` / `EMI From…`, and Haier 622L fridge sample has **no EMI line at all** (`01-current-state/product-page-variations.md:55`) with no verdict. No bank list, no tenure picker, no eligibility explanation, no link to EMI Bank List footer (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:22`). User cannot answer "can I finance 1.38L fridge at 3,800/month?"
- **Why It Is Relevant:** Transcom homepage already signals `Emi Bank List` (`page-analysis.md:22`) and `EMI36` exists — vocabulary is established but execution is partial. BD EMI is *high-friction* (paper form + bank conversion delay) so transparency must be *upstream* on PDP, not at paywall. Finding pairs with OPP-09 True Cost row (`03-opportunities/opportunity-pool.md:139-149`) — tenure picker (3/6/12/24/36) re-calculates monthly at price exposure.
- **Applicability:** **HIGH** — highest ROI for Tk 50k–1.5L. PDP ownership row: `Upfront + Energy + Install → EMI from Tk Y/month × N @ Bank [choose bank/tenure]` + explicit ineligibility verdict `EMI not available for this SKU → see EMI Bank List` (adapts Daraz Dhaka-only truthfulness). Show Pickaboo-style PDP tenure badge `Upto 36 months EMI` only when eligible. Suppress for Trimmer/TV low-ticket (Tk 3k flat — consistent with Pickaboo threshold).
- **Classification:** **INDUSTRY STANDARD** for Bangladesh electronics (Pickaboo/Daraz prove 0%-36m is hygiene). Transcom's inconsistency is the gap, not EMI invention.

## Finding 4 — COD + bKash/Nagad + Card-on-Delivery Plural Payment Row

- **Platform:** Daraz, Pickaboo, Chaldal, Rokomari across Bangladesh — unified via Levree 2026 market data (COD 75–90%) [WS]
- **Market Context:** Bangladesh payment mix is COD-dominant with mobile wallets as second rail. Levree: *"COD is still how most people pay"*; specialist platforms list `Free Shipping*, bKash, Card on Delivery, Cash on Delivery, Countrywide Delivery` as co-equal (Pickaboo footer). Rokomari pioneered COD for books to create trust before online payment adoption; Chaldal retains COD + online + bKash + card on delivery; Pickaboo lists COD, online banking, 0% EMI, debit/credit, bKash/Nagad, **Swipe/Card on Delivery** as parallel options. Daraz/Pickaboo both support COD nationwide (Daraz explicitly: all 64 districts via Daraz Express 70% parcels).
- **Pattern:** Checkout payment selector shows *plural rows* side-by-side: `◉ Cash on Delivery` / `◉ Card on Delivery (Swipe)` / `◉ bKash / Nagad (Mobile Wallet)` / `◉ Online Card (EMI eligible)` — not a single default. Each row shows availability by pincode/district (`Confirm COD availability for your specific district` advisory — COD coverage maps are not publicly detailed outside Dhaka). PDP preview echoes `Secure Payment System` trust bar (`page-analysis.md:34`) at price context.
- **Problem Solved:** Current checkout is undiscoverable (`CHECKOUT-01 P1` `02-ux-audit/issue-register.md:38` + `01-current-state/user-journeys.md:126-131` empty-cart gate). Before reaching it, PDP gives no payment-method scent — only `Avail Bank EMI` for cardholders. `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:34` `Secure Payment System` is homepage-only; not echoed on PDP/Cart per `TRUST-01 P2` (`02-ux-audit/issue-register.md:35`). COD-sensitive buyers цен assume online-only.
- **Why It Is Relevant:** Dhaka vs non-Dhaka split (Levree: non-Dhaka delivery 5–8 days) means COD assurance must be shown *before* Checkout: district-level COD availability + `No additional charges for COD` + `Pay with bKash at delivery` reduces abandonment for wallet-preferred segment. Like Pickaboo's "various methods... for your best convenience" framing, choice itself is trust.
- **Applicability:** **HIGH** — render plural payment row on PDP beneath EMI row (Finding 3) and on Cart Order Summary (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:161` currently `Subtotal ৳0 Total 0` hygiene) as `Cash on Delivery ✓ Deliverable area | bKash ✓ | Card on Delivery ✓`. District-aware: `Delivery to Rajshahi: COD available` fed from same zone matrix as Finding 5. No backend beyond payment-method availability per zone.
- **Classification:** **INDUSTRY STANDARD** in Bangladesh — failing to show COD/bKash alongside EMI is exclusionary for 75–90% of buyers.

## Finding 5 — Pincode/District Serviceability Checker + Fast Delivery Tag (No Permission Gate)

- **Platform:** Daraz Fast Delivery (nationwide launch 14 Aug 2026) + Flipkart × India Post 1.6L offices + Flipkart 100% serviceable pincodes (19k+ pincodes) [WS]
- **Market Context:** Bangladesh has 64 districts with uneven courier economics; rural pincodes/mauzas have sparse formal codes — area/district text is more reliable than pincode alone. Daraz solved historically by building **Daraz Express** (70% parcels) + **Choice single-warehouse** (1–3 day) and now **Fast Delivery** (Aug 2026): ~50k products across 2,500 sellers carry a `Fast Delivery` tag + search filter, earned via proven fulfilment performance. Flipkart parallel: reached 100% serviceable pincodes early, then partnered India Post for last-mile COD reach; Flipkart Authorised model document shows pincode enter executes verify at Order Summary.
- **Pattern:** Single text input on PDP: `Enter area / district / pincode` + `Check` (not browser geolocation permission). Response card: `✓ Deliverable to 1212 Dhaka — Delivery 2–4 days — Delivery fee Tk 0/Free — Installation Free/Bracket extra — Nearest pickup: Dhanmondi 1.2km (in stock)`. Persists in `sessionStorage` and re-renders in Cart landed-cost breakdown. Tag `Fast Delivery` on search PLP (`Show 12` + filter at `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:85-86`) lets buyers browse only quickly-deliverable items. Tag is *earned* (seller performance), not bought.
- **Problem Solved:** PDP today forces `Home Delivery Enable your Location` + `Store Pickup Enable your Location` on *every* sample (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:125`, `01-current-state/product-page-variations.md:59` — both gated). `PDP-02 P1` (`02-ux-audit/issue-register.md:27`): users cannot answer "deliverable to my area, by when, at what fee?" before price commitment; failure surfaces only at checkout address validation (`03-opportunities/opportunity-pool.md:7` OPP-01).
- **Why It Is Relevant:** Transcom Store Locator (`/store-locator` `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:199-204` List/Map, district dropdown, `Schedule your visit`) already models district-level fulfillment truth — same data drives estimator. `Enable your Location` gate is globally familiar but *regionally inappropriate* where location permission is low-trust and pincode literacy is low; district/area text has higher completion. Fast Delivery tag mirrors Pickaboo `3 Hours Express` and Daraz Choice `Free on 3+ items` — deal-sensitive buyers filter before browsing.
- **Applicability:** **HIGH** — replace gate with `Enter area/district + Check` on all 101 PDPs (flat namespace `00-input/sitemap-analysis.md:27`). AC/Washer/Fridge first (highest weight failure). Wire to Cart as `Landed cost: Product + Delivery + Install → Total`. Add PLP filter `Fast Delivery` alongside existing `Brand/Price/Display Size` (`01-current-state/product-discovery.md:79`) — consistent with Daraz filter pattern.
- **Classification:** **EMERGING** in Bangladesh (Daraz Fast Delivery Aug 2026; not yet universal); **INDUSTRY STANDARD** in India. Gap is competitive window.

## Finding 6 — Assisted Commerce / Phygital Experience Hubs for Tier-2/3

- **Platform:** Flipkart Authorised BuyZone + Styline Experience Hubs + Star Tech 20+ stores — verified [WS]
- **Market Context:** Tier-2/3 cities and non-metro districts show high *eagerness but low confidence* in self-serve e-commerce (Flipkart SVP quote: "high level of trust with neighbourhood trade stores"). Flipkart Authorised BuyZone: **10,000 general trade stores across 700 cities** hand-hold first-time buyers for electronics/mobiles — witnessed **5× growth**, **8× spike in TV/appliances**, 1 in 10 mobiles sold via BuyZone during festive. Styline Bangladesh (modest fashion marketplace) opened **5 experience hubs** (Shyamoli, Jamuna Future Park, Sylhet, Chittagong) explicitly as entry for customers "not accustomed to online or have limited faith in online sellers." Star Tech parallel: **20+ physical stores** in Dhaka, Chattogram, Rangpur, Gazipur, Khulna etc. — `64-district nationwide delivery` + 15 service centers as physical trust proxy.
- **Pattern:** Online product replicated in local trade store: assisted seller shows PDP, validates spec, collects order, handles OTP/install booking, buyer pays at store or COD. Hubs allow *physical check then online order* (or offline order). Transcom analogue: Store Locator (`/store-locator` `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:199-204` 4 districts observed: Sirajgonj, Kishorgonj, Chittagong, Sylhet) currently renders cards address/hours/`Schedule your visit` + Mapbox — but *not linked* from PDP/store-pickup choice, and no capability offered.
- **Problem Solved:** Homepage/pDP assume autonomous digital journey (search → PLP filters → PDP → Add To Cart). For high-consideration Tk 80k+ TV/AC/Fridge, Tier-2/3 buyer needs human reassurance + physical dimension/doorway validation (Finding 3–5 prerequisites) before ordering. `PDP-03 P1 Installation absent` and `PDP-01 inspection unsupported` (`02-ux-audit/issue-register.md:26-28`) compound self-serve gap.
- **Why It Is Relevant:** Transcom *already* has store footprint (footer Store Locator link); exchange TRUST-01 notes trust not echoed at PDP. Nearest-pickup alternative from Finding 5 is natural bridge: `Not home-deliverable but available at Star-Tech-like pickup: X km` with `Schedule your visit` CTA. This reuses `Schedule your visit` button (`page-analysis.md:201-204`) but wires it to product context, not store browsing alone.
- **Applicability:** **MEDIUM** — phase 1 is linkage (PDP `Store Pickup → show nearest 3 stores with stock + Schedule Visit` from existing locator), no trade-store network required. Phase 2 (BuyZone-like assisted) is ops partnership — defer until P0 checker confirms demand geography. Suppress for low-ticket Personal Care where self-serve suffices.
- **Classification:** **DIFFERENTIATOR** in Bangladesh electronics (marketplaces cannot replicate owned-store assisted sale credibly; Transcom/Star Tech/Pickaboo with showrooms can). Globally niche outside India EA model.

## Finding 7 — Social Commerce & Conversational Care (Facebook/Messenger/WhatsApp Human Agent)

- **Platform:** Facebook Commerce (BD thousands of exclusive sellers) + ChalDal Premium Care + Rokomari chat support — verified [WS]
- **Market Context:** Facebook Commerce is "massive, real channel" in Bangladesh — Levree 2026: *thousands of businesses sell exclusively through FB pages/groups, many buyers make regular purchases there.* Distinct traits: **zero standardized buyer protection**, COD depends on seller, dispute via DNCRP slow — yet it persists because **social influence** is decisive: 2024 Daraz Kushtia study finds `social influence β=0.131 p=0.03` positive on purchase intention; `perceived trust β=0.44` and `customer reviews β=0.41` dominate; `word-of-mouth significantly influences order procedures` (BJMS 2025 n=150). ChalDal Premium Care addresses same need: *dedicated personal shopping assistant via WhatsApp/call* for busy professionals — agent handles orders, tracks delivery, resolves post-delivery via message (`Register → agent calls within 24h → message agent to order`).
- **Pattern:** E-commerce site embeds conversational layer: (a) PDP `Share` + `Ask to buy via Messenger/WhatsApp` deep-link, (b) persistent `Chat with us / Need help? Click Here` (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:14` sticky trigger) that escalates to *human* WhatsApp/Messenger agent (not bot-only), (c) Premium-Care style *personal manager* for high-ticket large appliances (optional opt-in after PDP view). Reviews/testimonials displayed as social proof cards ("As seen on Facebook — 4.8★ 2.3k orders").
- **Problem Solved:** `Need help? Click Here ` is present on every page (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:14` OBSERVED) but does *not* open conversational channel in audit; footer hints `Chat With Us` / `Connect with us` social icons (`page-analysis.md:18-26`) are unvalidated; no WhatsApp/Messenger PDP deep-link. `PDP-07 P1` review absence means social proof is empty. Social commerce buyers check Messenger before site.
- **Why It Is Relevant:** For Bangladesh trust-sensitive buyer, *human chat* is trust — not FAQ. Integrating WhatsApp/Messenger alongside hotline (Finding 9) meets reality where deal discovery happens on Facebook/Group chat before site visit. BRAC/Daraz research confirms social influence is independent driver (interaction effect: privacy × social influence enhances purchase).
- **Applicability:** **HIGH** — PDP `Wishlist/Compare/Share` trio (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:129-130`) should add `Share on WhatsApp` + `Chat with agent` alongside `Share`. `Need help?` should resolve to WhatsApp deep-link + hotline 16212 fallback. Premium Care opt-in for >Tk 50k: `Get personal manager for this fridge — message on WhatsApp`. Lightweight: deep-links + prefilled product URL, no platform build.
- **Classification:** **INDUSTRY STANDARD** as social layer in Bangladesh (absence would be conspicuous given FB Commerce scale); conversational agent follow-through is **EMERGING** (ChalDal-level). Globally, headless social share is standard — *human* manager assignment is differentiator.

## Finding 8 — 14-Day Returns + Cross-Category Doorstep Exchange with AI Diagnostics

- **Platform:** Daraz 14-day return (ALL products, expanded 2025 from Mall-only) + Flipkart ReCommerce AI Exchange (26 categories) + Trust Shield post-purchase — verified [WS]
- **Market Context:** Bangladesh post-Evaly trust collapse made "what if I regret?" existential. Daraz explicitly extended return window to 14 days *across entire platform* (not just Mall) as *trust-building measure* (Ben Yi interview, TBS 2026) — reason: "past market incidents made many consumers cautious." Flipkart ReCommerce simultaneously launched **10-step AI diagnostics** assessing *internal functionality + external condition* within minutes, competitive exchange value, *cross-category* (refrigerator → washing machine, mobile → laptop, even fan/microwave → purifier/geyser) — flexibility framed as "idle devices as currency at home." Exchange flow: Product page `Exchange Offer` visible → brand/model/condition → instant value applied → delivery with **doorstep inspection + OTP confirmation**.
- **Pattern:** Two surfaces: (a) Return policy shown *at PDP price block* (`7-day` for Choice, `14-day for ALL` for Daraz standard), (b) Exchange value calculator embedded on PDP (like `Get Exchange up to 12000 Tk` on PLP card `01-current-state/product-discovery.md:119-120` but generic — vs Flipkart precise valuation). Cross-category exchange means low-ticket old appliance can fund high-ticket new.
- **Problem Solved:** Transcom Exchange hub `/exchange` exists (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:208-212` VERIFIED — 2 cards: Refrigerator/Washing Machine → parameterized flow `/exchange/{id}?category=...`) and PLP `Get Exchange up to 12000 Tk` is visible, but valuation/execution remains opaque — no per-SKU exchange estimator on PDP, no AI-diagnostics flow, no condition/OTP step documented. `01-current-state/ecommerce-capabilities.md:52` notes Exchange hub VERIFIED but detail NOT TESTED. User cannot project net price.
- **Why It Is Relevant:** Daraz 14-day move is baseline — buyers who experience it expect it elsewhere. `TRUST-01` homepage `Free Installation / Original Product / Exchange Program` must persist to PDP — 14-day return badge at price moment is stronger than generic trust bar. Cross-category exchange converts idle BD households (common second fridge/washer) into demand — particularly for AC/TV upgrades.
- **Applicability:** **MEDIUM** — PDP add `14-Day Hassle-Free Return ✓` badge next to `In stock` (`01-current-state/product-page-variations.md:58`) + `Exchange value: Estimate up to Tk 12k → doorstep inspection → OTP` beneath price. Requires return policy content (already hinted footer) and exchange valuation feed — start with static "up to" + inspection truth, evolve to AI diagnostics. Related to OPP-12 installation booking as post-purchase linked service.
- **Classification:** **EMERGING** in Bangladesh (Daraz 14-day is 2025 change; cross-category AI diagnostics not yet BD-standard). In India fast-becoming **INDUSTRY STANDARD** for large appliances.

## Finding 9 — Hotline-Centric Support Spine (16212/16492/16710) + Schedule Store Visit

- **Platform:** Transcom 16212 + Daraz 16492 + ChalDal 16710 + Star Tech 16793 — hotline pattern unified [WS]
- **Market Context:** Bangladesh buyer exhibits **support dependency** — phone remains primary reassurance channel for high-consideration. Chaldal explicitly positions hotline as order alternative: *"Can I order over the phone? Absolutely. Our hotline is 16710 — too busy to place order, we handle it."* Daraz EMI conversion *requires calling 16492* before rider form. Transcom footer `9:00 AM–9:00 PM @ 16212` (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:18-19`) and homepage 14.6% premium? Not; but support expectation is "call human now" before cart. Store Locator (`01-current-state/page-analysis.md:199-204` 4 cards: name/address/hours `Saturday–Thursday 9:30 AM–9:00 PM` + `Schedule your visit`) is another human touchpoint — distinct from `Track Order Status / Track Your Service` header links (`page-analysis.md:7`).
- **Pattern:** Every page exposes **tel hotline as primary CTA**, not buried footer link. PLP/PDP sticky footer or header shows `Call 16212 9AM–9PM` with tel link + `Need help? ## Cross-Finding Insights (Regional Lens)

1. **Trust is not branding — it is mechanics.** Findings 1, 2, 8, 9 together form Bangladesh trust stack: Mall/Verified badge (who sold it), Open-Box + OTP (what you received), 14-day return (what if regret), hotline + store (who to call). Transcom homepage `Free Installation / Original Product / Exchange Program / Secure Payment` bar (`page-analysis.md:34`) claims these — regional leaders *prove* each claim at PDP/Cart moment.
2. **Financing + delivery + install are one row, not three pages.** Findings 3, 4, 5, 8 converge into OPP-09 True Cost row (`03-opportunities/opportunity-pool.md:139-149`): `Upfront + Delivery + Install + Energy → EMI/month`. Bangladesh buyer decides on *monthly affordability* and *deliverability*, not sticker price alone. Pickaboo/Daraz EMI form reality makes this more — not less — important.
3. **COD + bKash is not an alternative — it is the default.** Finding 4 refuses global "card-first checkout" assumption. Bangladesh 75–90% COD + mobile wallet dominance (Levree) means EMI row (Finding 3) must coexist with `Cash on Delivery ✓` — not replace it. Dual-mode (finance vs cash) is regional hygiene.
4. **Location permission is anti-pattern here.** Finding 5 explicitly rejects global "share location" gate (`Enable your Location` `01-current-state/page-analysis.md:125` P1) in favour of district/area text — higher completion, no permission anxiety, matches 64-district mental model already in Store Locator.
5. **Human is channel, not fallback.** Findings 6, 7, 9 share premise: trade store, Messenger/WhatsApp agent, hotline 16212 are *preferred* paths for Tier-2/3 and high-ticket, not escalation after site failure. `Need help? Click Here` (`page-analysis.md:14`) + footer icons must resolve to *human* within 2 taps.
